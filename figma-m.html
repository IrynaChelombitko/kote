Day by day
Tap a day on the profile or in the list.
Day 19.5 km, +868 m
Lyngseidet to Fastdalsbu
Boat drop at 08:00, then birch forest and the first moraine. Short day to check boots and packs.
Day 211.2 km, +1,390 m
Fastdalsbu to Isskardhytta
Up the Fastdal glacier on a rope, crampons from the first ice. Night in a self-service hut.
Day 312.4 km, +1,272 m
Isskardhytta to Bivouac at 1380 m
Summit day. Exposed ridge to point 1520, turnaround at 13:00 whatever the weather looks like.
Day 410.8 km, +546 m
Bivouac to Jægervatn
Long descent on old snow, then the first river crossing on foot. Sauna at the lake.
Day 59.6 km, +337 m
Jægervatn to Koppangen
Coastal path under the cliffs. This is also the spare day if the ridge was closed.
Day 68.5 km, +437 m
Koppangen to Lyngseidet by boat
Last ridge, last view, boat back across the fjord at 17:30.
