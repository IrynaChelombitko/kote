Small guided groups
on hard routes
in the far North

Kote is a concept booking platform for multi-day treks in Norway, Sweden, Iceland, the Faroes and Svalbard. Eight travellers, two guides and one spare day per departure. Grades are counted in metres of ascent and kilos on your back, so people choose a route they can finish.

TravellerFinds a route by grade, picks a departure by the seats left, books with a €400 deposit and gets ready with the guide.
Agency managerFills departures, chases balances, reads the fitness answers before the guide calls.

The number in the logo is a spot height, like the marks on a topographic map. It always shows the highest point of what you are looking at.

Four questions
before a hard trek

People who book a grade 5 route are not short of motivation. They are short of certainty. Each step of the product answers the one question that stops them at that moment.
Choose
Am I fit enough for this?
Grades are written in metres of ascent per day and kilos carried, not in adjectives. Every route shows its elevation profile.
Grade 5: up to 1,600 m a day, 16 kg

Will the group actually go?
Eight squares per departure, one per place. Orange means two or fewer are left, so the decision has a deadline you can see.

What do I carry, and who checks me?
A 31-item kit list with rentals, and a call with your guide eight weeks before. If the grade looks wrong, you move to an easier route for free.

Every plan has a spare day and a turnaround time. My trips shows the meeting point, the balance date and your highest summit so far.
