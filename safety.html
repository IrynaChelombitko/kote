8 weeks before
The fitness call
Your guide calls you for twenty minutes and goes through your last hikes. If the grade looks wrong for you, we move you to an easier route at no cost.
The evening before
Kit check and briefing
Every pack is opened on the floor of the hut or the guesthouse. Missing kit is rented on the spot, so nobody starts without crampons or a shell.
Every summit day
A turnaround time
13:00 on Kebnekaise and Lyngen, 14:00 on Vågakallen. The guide turns the group at that time whatever the weather looks like.
Every route
One spare day
Each plan has a day with nothing in it. If a ridge or a ford is closed, we use it. If not, it becomes a short day.
On the ice
Two ropes, always
On glaciers the group walks as two rope teams of five, each with a guide and a full crevasse rescue kit.
Every night on Svalbard
Bear watch
Camp sits inside an alarm fence, and someone is awake. Two hours each, guides included.
If something goes wrong
Satellite and helicopter
Each group carries a satellite messenger and checks in every evening. Rescue insurance covers a helicopter up to €100,000.
The spare day is the one Rype likes best. Most groups end up using it for sleep.
